    How it works

    A simple, transparent verification pipeline.

    1

    Upload

    Select a supported image from your laptop or phone.

    2

    Validate

    The backend checks the file type and size.

    3

    Analyze

    The TensorFlow Lite detector evaluates the image.

    4

    Report

    The actual model output becomes the risk score, verdict and evidence.

    How to Use CyberLens

    Follow these simple steps.

    1

    Open CyberLens on your laptop or phone.

    2

    Choose a JPG, PNG, JPEG or WEBP image.

    3

    Click Analyze file.

    4

    Wait for the AI model to finish.

    5

    Read the risk score and verdict.

    6

    Review the evidence before making important decisions.
